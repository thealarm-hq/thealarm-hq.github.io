Alarm clock for iPhone

Alarms for any day.

Coming soon to the App Store

What The Alarm does

Like a calendar

Alarms for any day

See every alarm of the month at a glance – and sleep in on public holidays and during your vacation without touching a thing.

  • Public holidays for more than 200 countries and their regions – search your city and The Alarm picks the right ones
  • Vacation entered by hand or taken from your calendar
  • Change a single day, or that day and all that follow – with Undo
  • Month and week: when it rings and when you can sleep in
  • Every alarm time explained – why it rings, or why it stays quiet

Every rule you need

Weekly, in a rhythm or on set dates

Set alarms the way your weeks really are – every week, every few days, on the first Monday of the month or only on the dates you pick.

  • Weekdays, rhythms like “4 on, 4 off” and monthly rules
  • Groups, notes and templates to start from
  • Share an alarm as a QR code or file – it arrives ready to save
  • By the sun or by your first event of the day (Pro)
  • Describe it in your own words – Apple Intelligence fills in the alarm on your iPhone (Pro)

For shift work

Shifts in their rhythm

Early, late, night: set up your shift plan once, and The Alarm rings at the right time for every shift – around public holidays and vacation.

  • Twelve shift systems to start with, or your own shifts with wake-up time, start and end
  • Snap a photo of your roster or open the PDF – Apple Intelligence reads it on your iPhone
  • Share your shift plan with colleagues as a link, QR code or file
  • Shift count with Sundays, public holidays and short breaks between shifts

Shift plans come with Pro. Compare Free and Pro

Sleep

Enough sleep – even around night shifts

The Alarm knows when you have to get up and shows how much sleep is left. Around night shifts, it plans a nap before the first night and a short sleep after the last.

  • Sleep in the week view, from bedtime to your alarm
  • A bedtime reminder and “Good night” with the time left to sleep
  • Sleep plan around night shifts, with a nap and a short sleep (Pro)
  • Sleep sounds that fade out before your next alarm (Pro)

Good morning

Wake up gently – and really get up

A sunrise on your nightstand, a sound you like and a check that you're awake – mornings that start well.

  • 15 alarm sounds of its own, made in the app
  • Safety net and evening check: a notification with every alarm, and every evening the first alarm of tomorrow
  • Your own sounds: record, import or load by link, then trim (Pro)
  • Nightstand clock with a sunrise, wake-up light and sleep sounds (Pro)
  • Wake-up check, snooze budget and snooze coach (Pro)
  • Good-morning card and a morning routine on the Lock Screen (Pro)

More than alarms

World clock, stopwatch and timer

Cities and people with their time, public holidays and daylight – plus a stopwatch with laps and as many timers as you need.

  • Slide through the day to see what time it is everywhere
  • Any city in the world – with its public holidays, weather and sunrise
  • Stopwatch with laps that marks the fastest and slowest, several timers at once
  • Several stopwatches side by side – start, stop and sort each one (Pro)
  • The stopwatch on the Lock Screen with Lap and Stop – or take laps with the Action Button
  • Share a timer or the stopwatch – the timer rings for both of you at the same moment

Always at hand

The Alarm works where you need it – often without opening the app.

  • Widgets

    Your next alarm, the next day to sleep in and a month calendar on the Home Screen.

  • Lock Screen and StandBy

    The next alarm on the Lock Screen and big on your nightstand.

  • Live Activities

    Alarms and timers counting down, and your morning routine step by step.

  • Controls and Action button

    Skip the next alarm, take a power nap or stay home sick – in one tap.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Say “Good night in The Alarm” or build your own shortcuts.

  • Search and Focus

    Find your alarms in Spotlight and filter them by Focus.

Your alarms stay yours.

No account, no ads, no tracking. What you enter stays on your iPhone.

  • On your iPhone

    Alarms, shift plans, places and sounds stay on your device – they never reach us.

  • No tracking

    No ads, no profile of you, no data sold. Anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off, show us what to improve.

  • App lock

    Face ID or your own code – widgets and Spotlight then show no names.

  • Backups where you want

    In iCloud Drive or any folder in Files, encrypted with a password if you like.
